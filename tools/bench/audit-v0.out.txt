
## 1. f64 ulp at deep time (DEC-014 docs say 7.8 ms at 1e6 yr)
secondsPerYear          = 31557600 (exact even integer: true)
1e6 years in seconds    = 31557600000000  (2^44=17592186044416, 2^45=35184372088832)
ulp(1e6 yr)             = 0.00390625 s = 3.9063 ms
t * Number.EPSILON      = 0.007007194824382168 s  ← THIS is how you get ~7 ms
ulp is 2^(floor(log2(x))-52), NOT x*EPSILON.
x*EPSILON overestimates by ~2× in the lower half of a binade.
VERDICT: docs 7.8 ms is WRONG. Code comment 3.9 ms is RIGHT.
ulp(1e9 yr)             = 4 s = 4.000 s
1/60 at 1e6 yr in ulps  = 4.2667
round(1/60) at 1e6 yr   = 0.015625 s = 1/64 = 0.015625
